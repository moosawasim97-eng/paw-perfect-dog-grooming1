Welcome to Paw Perfect

Not all professional dog groomers are created equal.

We offer a calm and relaxed one-to-one grooming service that is convenient and flexible to fit around you. Travelling to dog groomers can take up valuable time and produce unnecessary stress for your dog. Never worry about having a muddy car or relying on public transport again, and have us come to you!

You can be reassured that Paw Perfect has a commitment to ongoing training and qualifications to the highest standard. We ensure your dog has our full, individual attention, which is just one of the reasons our service is unique.

Why choose us?

The proof is in the pampering

The comfort of your pet is most important to me. We create a peaceful and safe environment whilst your dog is in our care. There’s no separation anxiety or physical stress for your dog, as there’s no need to leave its familiar home environment.

We take the Animal Welfare Act’s five freedoms seriously, and always look for ways to reduce stress and create a bond with your beloved pooch.
