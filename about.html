About us

Welcome to Paw Perfect

Not all professional dog groomers are created equal.

We offer a calm and relaxed one-to-one grooming service that is convenient and flexible to fit around you. Travelling to dog groomers can take up valuable time and produce unnecessary stress for your dog. Never worry about having a muddy car or relying on public transport again, and have us come to you!

You can be reassured that Paw Perfect has a commitment to ongoing training and qualifications to the highest standard. We ensure your dog has our full, individual attention, which is just one of the reasons our service is unique.

Our timeline

  1. 2010

    Entrepreneurial at 16, with a successful dog walking and pet sitting business in the West End of London, alongside studying a Level 3 Extended Diploma in Animal Management.

  2. 2012 to 2018

    Graduated with the highest grade in the Level 3 City & Guilds Dog Grooming qualification, gaining six years of experience throughout. Before setting up Paw Perfect, I was Head Groomer in prestige Chelsea-based parlours, providing excellent customer service for celebrities and notable professionals.

  3. 2018

    Paw Perfect was established in 2018 by Star, as a London-wide independent pet grooming and services business.

  4. Now

    Pets groomed at Paw Perfect are treated as my own and never rushed, to ensure the trust of the animal and owner. Attending seminars and continuing training keeps me up to date, so your pet receives the highest standard of care.
